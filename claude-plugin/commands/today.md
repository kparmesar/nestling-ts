description: Show your baby's day in Nestling
argument-hint: "[day, for example yesterday]"
---

Show the day in Nestling for: $ARGUMENTS

If no day is given, use today. Call `show_day` for that date. Where the day card cannot be shown, give a short summary from `get_day_summary`: total sleep, feeds, nappies, and the time of the last feed.
